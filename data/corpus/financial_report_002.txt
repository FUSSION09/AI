SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
DIVISION OF ENFORCEMENT - COMPLIANCE AUDIT REPORT

Target Entity: Meridian Trust Holdings
Related Entities: Vanguard Horizon Ltd, Apex Capital Group, Helena Vance
Date: November 2024

FINDINGS OF INVESTIGATION:
1. Meridian Trust Holdings maintains undocumented correspondent accounts in Zurich and Panama City.
2. Helena Vance authorized a $450,000 disbursement categorized as consulting retainers, conflicting with 15 U.S.C. 78dd-1 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act).
3. Cross-entity auditing confirms that Vanguard Horizon Ltd transferred funds directly into offshore shell accounts controlled by Marcus Vance.
4. The compliance committee recommends full disclosure under SEC regulations and prompt referral to FinCEN.
