UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
SOUTHERN DISTRICT OF NEW YORK

Case No. 24-CR-8891
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA v. VANGUARD HORIZON LTD and MARCUS VANCE

OPINION AND ORDER:
Defendant Marcus Vance served as Chief Operating Officer of Vanguard Horizon Ltd, a financial services entity registered in Delaware. Between January 2024 and October 2024, Marcus Vance directed multiple wire transfers of $9,800 to avoid currency transaction reporting limits. 

The Court finds that Vanguard Horizon Ltd violated 31 U.S.C. 5324 and 18 U.S.C. 1956 through deliberate structuring and layering across affiliated entities, including Apex Capital Group and Meridian Trust. The Court concludes that the executive engaged in willful non-compliance and orders an asset freeze.
